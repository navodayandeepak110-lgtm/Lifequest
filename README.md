LifeQuest-
LifeQuest is a habit tracker designed to help you build a more intentional, balanced, and productive life. It gives you a simple place to record daily habits, review how your day went, and turn your long-term ambitions into achievable goals.
Track Your Daily Habits
Keep track of the routines that matter most to you, whether they are related to health, learning, work, mindfulness, or personal growth.
- Create custom habits that fit your lifestyle
- Mark habits as completed each day
- Build consistency through streak tracking
- Set flexible daily, weekly, or monthly targets
- Focus on small actions that lead to meaningful progress
  
Analyze Your Day
LifeQuest helps you look beyond simple checkmarks and understand how you spend your time and energy.
- Review which habits you completed each day
- Identify patterns in your productivity and motivation
- Reflect on what went well and what could improve
- Add notes, moods, or daily reflections
- Spot obstacles that may be affecting your routine
  
Set Goals With Purpose
Turn broad aspirations into clear, practical milestones. LifeQuest helps you stay connected to the bigger reason behind your daily habits.
- Create short-term and long-term goals
- Break large goals into manageable tasks
- Link habits to the goals they support
- Track progress over time
- Celebrate milestones along the way
  
Stay Motivated
Progress is easier to maintain when it is visible. LifeQuest makes your growth feel tangible and rewarding.
- View completion rates and streaks
- Monitor weekly and monthly progress
- See how consistent effort adds up
- Receive encouragement to stay on track
- Adjust goals when your priorities change
  
Build a Better Routine
LifeQuest is not about perfection—it is about learning, improving, and making steady progress every day. Whether you want to exercise more, read consistently, improve your focus, sleep better, or develop a new skill, the app gives you the structure to make those changes stick.
